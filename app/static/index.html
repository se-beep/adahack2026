How to register with a GP

A GP is your local doctor. Registering is free. You can register if you will live here for more than 3 months.

  1. Pick a GP practice near where you live. A practice only takes people who live in its area, so ask if your address is covered.
  2. Fill in the registration form. You can get it from the practice or from NHS inform.
  3. Hand it in or email it to the practice. They may ask for ID and proof of your address. They should not turn you away if you do not have these.
  4. The practice will phone or email you when you are registered.

        How to get a Young Scot card

        The card is free if you are aged 11 to 25 and live in Scotland.

        1. Apply online at getyournec.scot. You will need a photo of your face and proof of who you are, like a passport.
        2. Or apply in person at an Edinburgh library. Book a time with the library first.
        3. Your card comes in the post.
        Apply at getyournec.scot

        What your card gets you

        • Free bus travel if you are under 22. Ask for it when you apply for your card.
        • Money off in shops, cafes and online. Show your card or use the Young Scot app.
        • Proof of age. The card has the PASS logo, so it works as ID.
        • Rewards. Collect points on young.scot and swap them for prizes.
        • £1 entry to Historic Scotland and National Trust for Scotland places.
        • Cheaper trains if you are 16, 17 or 18.
        See all discounts on young.scot

        Lost your card?

        1. Ask for a new one online at getyournec.scot. You do not need to fill in the whole form again.
        2. Or go to a library and ask for a replacement Young Scot card. Phone first to book a time.
        3. Need help? Email one-edinburgh@edinburgh.gov.uk
